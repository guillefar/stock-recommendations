### 1. The audit (read-only, before any code)

Eight scheduled runs 07-15 → 07-29, 9 `run_metrics` rows, 4,173 graded outcomes, 3 `prediction_patterns` sets, 2 retros.

**Finding 1 — the grading bands were single-stock bands applied to every instrument.**

| | ETF | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| Median 30d absolute move | **2.62%** | 10.70% |
| Mean 30d absolute move | 4.29% | 14.67% |
| % ever reaching the 10% `watch_move` | **13.6%** | 52.2% |
| % inside the 4% neutral band | **64.5%** | 25.2% |

`WATCH` grades INCORRECT below 4% and CORRECT only at ≥10%. An ETF's typical month is 2.6%. So **`WATCH×ETF` was arithmetically required to fail** — its 344 INCORRECT rows averaged a **1.66%** move — and **`HOLD×ETF` was required to succeed** (HOLD is CORRECT when flat; ETFs are flat). The miner had found both, promoted them to CONFIRMED at 0.91–0.95 over three Fridays, and **since 07-20 the s25 gate had been injecting them into all 63 ticker prompts**, instructing the model to stop issuing WATCH on ETFs. Ten days of prompts steered by a bug.

**Finding 2 — confidence is not informative, and the band bug had hidden that too.** Pre-regrade calibration looked textbook-monotone (27.1 / 40.6 / 59.3 across <0.40 / 0.40–0.59 / 0.60–0.79) and had been mined as "Confianza 0.60–0.79: zona dorada" (CONFIRMED 0.89, **injected**). Post-regrade it is flat — 64.8 / 57.5 / 61.9 / 57.1 overall, and flat *within stocks* (64.0 / 60.7 / 56.5) and *within ETFs* (65.5 / 53.3 / 63.0) separately. **All four top mined patterns were band artifacts.**

**Finding 3 — `BUY: 2.6%` is mostly market direction.** Every graded 30d outcome comes from recs generated 05-17 → 06-26, a window averaging **−4.80%** (June −7.24%); only 19.8% of outcomes rose >4%. SELL at 100% and BUY at 2.6% are one fact stated twice. Market-relative (excess vs same-day cohort): **SELL −11.0pp**, **AVOID −5.6pp** (genuine skill), HOLD +0.9pp, WATCH +0.3pp, **BUY −3.0pp** (mild real anti-skill). The miner's "BUY es un lastre: 0% en todas las muestras materiales" (CONFIRMED 0.88, rank 5) overstates a small real effect and sits one reshuffle from teaching the system never to buy.

**Finding 4 — 2 of 8 scheduled runs failed completely.** Fri **07-17** and Mon **07-27**: `Batch … still in_progress after 2700s — canceling` → all 63 tickers None, nothing persisted, exit 1 (`run_metrics` rows 4 and 8: `tickers_ok=0, tickers_failed=63`). Latency has a long tail — 07-20 took 37 min, 07-22 34 min (near-misses under the 45-min deadline).

**Finding 5 — the weekly steps ran on a dead run.** On 07-17, `tickers_ok=0`, yet steps 10/11 spent two Claude calls, wrote a `weekly_retrospectives` row for a week with no data, and appended a `prediction_patterns` row.

**Finding 6 — the loop's activation cost ~18%** ($0.098 → $0.115/run; batch input 94k → 127k tokens ≈ **+520 tokens/ticker**), because the gate passes full pattern `description`s including evidence strings like `"46C/288I en 334 decididas"`.

### 2. The fixes (user-approved after presenting the audit)

- **Asset-class band scaling** ([src/evaluate_outcomes.py](../src/evaluate_outcomes.py)): `ASSET_CLASS_BAND_SCALE = {"ETF": 0.30}` + new **`bands_for(horizon, quote_type)`**; `grade()` gains an optional 4th `quote_type` (default `None` → **unscaled**, so the index and the untyped tickers are unchanged and every pre-s26 test still holds verbatim). The 0.30 is the **measured** ETF/stock mean-absolute-move ratio and it is **stable across horizons** (0.317 at 7d, 0.292 at 30d) — one factor serves all five horizons and, being a uniform scale, preserves the base bands' √time shape and monotonicity. `_fetch_matured` now joins `tickers` for `quote_type`.
- **`--regrade` flag**: drops the `NOT EXISTS` "already graded" filter and leans on the existing `ON DUPLICATE KEY UPDATE` — upsert in place, **no delete, no id churn**.
- **Batch resilience** ([src/analysis/claude_client.py](../src/analysis/claude_client.py)): `BATCH_DEADLINE_SECONDS` **45 → 90 min**; new `BATCH_CANCEL_GRACE_SECONDS = 5 min` and **`_await_batch_end`** — on timeout the batch is still canceled, but then polled until `processing_status == "ended"` so its **already-completed requests are harvested** (results are only served once ended; that's the whole trick). A slow batch now costs the stragglers, not the run. All-None remains the fallback only if the cancel never settles.
- **Workflow ceiling** ([.github/workflows/run_recommendations.yml](../.github/workflows/run_recommendations.yml)): `timeout-minutes` **60 → 120** — it was already tighter than the batch tail (both failed runs burned 48 min). A test asserts the job ceiling stays above `BATCH_DEADLINE_SECONDS + BATCH_CANCEL_GRACE_SECONDS + 10`, so the two cannot drift apart.
- **Weekly-step gate** ([src/main.py](../src/main.py)): `weekly_ok = bool(all_recommendations)`; steps 10 and 11 require it. `--force-retro` / `--force-patterns` still override (ad-hoc backfills need them). One warning line when skipped.

## Invariants (don't break)

All of HANDOFF_25's invariants stand **except the retired cron-shed one** (see §0). New or changed this session:

- **Grading bands are per-horizon *and* per-asset-class.** Change `HORIZON_BANDS` or `ASSET_CLASS_BAND_SCALE` in [src/evaluate_outcomes.py](../src/evaluate_outcomes.py), never at call sites. `grade()`'s `quote_type` defaults to `None` = **unscaled** — that default is load-bearing for every pre-s26 test and for the index/untyped tickers.
- **The 0.30 is an empirical volatility ratio, not a knob.** It is pinned by a test. If you change it, re-derive it from `recommendation_outcomes` (mean absolute forward return, ETF vs non-ETF, per horizon) and re-grade.
- **Any band change requires a re-grade** (`--regrade`) with user sign-off, or the corpus carries mixed semantics. `--regrade` upserts; it never deletes.
- **`BATCH_DEADLINE_SECONDS + BATCH_CANCEL_GRACE_SECONDS` must stay comfortably under the workflow's `timeout-minutes`** — test-enforced. Raising one means raising the other.
- **A timed-out batch must still be awaited to "ended" before reading results** — that is what converts a lost run into a partial one. Don't "simplify" the cancel path back to an immediate all-None return.
- **Steps 10/11 require `tickers_ok > 0`** unless force-flagged. A run that persisted nothing has no week to review and must not append a pattern set.
- **A mined pattern is a hypothesis about the *metric*, not necessarily about the market.** Before acting on one (or hand-writing a prompt intervention from it), check whether the grading definition manufactures it. Session 26 exists because that check hadn't been made: four CONFIRMED patterns at 0.82–0.95 were all artifacts, and the s25 loop had been feeding them into production for ten days.
